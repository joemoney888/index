# Family A pre-run checks (23 Nov 2026, Claude Sonnet 5.5). Coverage, cost and timezone only. No strategy returns, signs or means of any traded rule were computed or looked at.

Data: OANDA NAS100 5-minute bars, sample 2021-05-20 to 2026-11-17 (1,434 weekdays).

1. **Calendar cross-check (bar existence only).** 66 weekdays have fewer than 78 bars from 09:30 to 16:00 New York. 63 are explained by famA/calendar.json (53 full holidays and 10 early closes inside the window; the 11th listed early close, 2026-11-27, is after the sample and the official NYSE page confirms it, with 2026-12-24 also an early close). All 10 early-close days have exactly 45 bars (the CFD stops at 13:00), so the early-close skip IS necessary. No calendar holiday has a full session of bars. Nothing missing from the calendar was found: no day with 45 bars or zero bars is absent from it. The remaining 3 low-bar days are data gaps: 2022-05-04 (64 bars, feed freeze around the FOMC announcement), 2022-12-13 (75 bars), 2026-02-04 (74 bars); the 78-bar test excludes them. The official NYSE page shows only 2026 to 2028; for 2021 to 2025 the evidence is the data cross-check above plus Claude's memory of the public calendar. A sample-year-by-year check against NYSE/ICE press releases remains possible but would change nothing unless a date differs.
2. **Sample size.** 1,371 trading days with the 09:30 to 16:00 session bar set after skips (1,369 have both the 15:30 and 15:55 bars needed for A6b).
3. **Spreads by 30-minute New York slot (basis points of price).** Median 0.37 to 0.40 bp (about 0.5 index points); 95th percentile 0.54 to 0.88 bp; maximum 5.8 bp; zero-spread bars: none. Bars above 10 times the slot median: 31 of 106,917, concentrated at 09:30 (20) and 13:30 (5); the largest is 5.8 bp. Decision: they are real wide-spread moments, not data errors, so they are KEPT and each bar pays its own half-spread, as the spec says.
4. **Timezone unit tests on real days.** 09:30 New York = 14:30 UTC on 2022-01-12 and 13:30 UTC on 2022-07-13; the same 13:30 UTC holds in the March and late-October weeks when the UK and US clocks differ (2023-03-15, 2023-10-30). The 15:55 bar closes 16:00 New York. The 09:10 New York bar exists on every sample day (0 missing); the 08:00 London bar is missing on 1 of 1,371 days (that day is skipped for A5 only).
5. **Spread of the last half hour (second moment only).** SD of the gross 15:30 to 16:00 return is 29.9 bp, above the 25 bp assumed in the spec. Detectable mean at t = 2.8 with n = 1,369 is 2.8 x 29.9 / sqrt(1369) = 2.3 bp (the spec said about 1.9 bp). No threshold changes; the +1.0 bp floor and t >= 2.8 stand. Used only for the power statement.
6. **Trial count pre-created before the run.** N: 941,542 + 3 = **941,545** registered here, before the runner exists. A recount from the run files gives 943,542 (see docs/HOUSEKEEPING_2026-11-23.md), so the conservative figure would be 943,545; the luck-bar difference is under 0.001.
