## 1. The project in one paragraph
A documented paper-trading / AI experiment. Goal: find a trading rule with real statistical evidence, being strict about not fooling ourselves. Two agents: **Blue Falcon** (older ORB breakout agent, private repo, first practice account, runs to about 24 Nov) and **Blue Falcon Green / BFG** (this public repo `joemoney888/index`; US-index mean-reversion rule; second practice account; GitHub Actions triggered every 5 minutes by cron-job.org). The owner is exploring using BFG's rule in a prop-firm challenge (FTMO 2-Step) as a "level 2" step, and is parking sponsorship / "platform for other people's trading ideas" ideas until results exist.

## 2. Research discipline (fixed before the runs, please audit these)
- Data: OANDA 5-minute bars (with real spread) for NAS100, SPX500, US30, GER40, UK100, GOLD, SILVER, EURUSD, GBPUSD, USDJPY, 2021-05-19 to 2026-11-18 (data window pinned in `research_fetch.py`).
- Split by time: TRAIN = everything older than 2 years; VALIDATION = the year before the last; EXAM = the final 365 days, locked and used ONCE per finalist set (NAS100's exam was used once).
- Every variant tried is counted (N). Candidate bar: train trades ≥500, mean ≥ +0.03R, day-clustered t ≥ sqrt(2 ln N)+0.5 ("luck bar"). Finalist: validation mean ≥ +0.03R, t ≥ 2.0, ≥150 trades. Exam pass: mean ≥ +0.05R and t ≥ 2.5 (3.0 in later notes). A pass only earns forward paper-trading.
- Engine: `agent/search.py` (features, random search, simulation with half-spread entry costs, stops/targets in multiples of 24h ATR, time exit, trailing stop, one position per market). Variants searched so far across families: well over 150,000 (ledgers in `search/*/runs`).
- Known weaknesses to check: ledger N across versions is conservative; day-clustered t may still be optimistic; the same data was used to pick the NAS100 rule's hyper-parameters (train+validation) and then the exam (1 year); a single instrument fit; stop fills in gaps are assumed at the stop price in the backtest (live may differ).

## 3. The BFG rule (public, `search/NAS100/finalists.json`)
Mean reversion toward fading extremes: score = weighted sum of 16 causal features (weights on position in 24h range −0.5, RSI 5m −1.0, RSI 1h −1.0, 14-bar stochastic −0.5; the rest 0); trade when |score| ≥ 0.47 inside UTC hours 13–21; only in the direction of the 20-day trend; gate: ATR(12 bars)/ATR(24h) > 1 (volatility expanding); entry next bar open ± half spread; stop 2.98×ATR(24h), target 3.79×ATR; time exit after 192 bars (16 h); trailing stop starts at 0.5 ATR in profit and trails 1.0 ATR behind best price; one position at a time per market; 1% account-risk sizing (about £5 on the small demo account).
Backtest (NAS100): train +0.042R (t 5.3, n 5,944), validation +0.033R (t 2.26, n 1,626), exam +0.067R (t 4.28, n 1,368). About 1,300–1,650 trades a year. Unfitted copies on SPX500 and US30 are about +0.015R (weak). Win rate about 56%; longest losing run 7–8; worst day about −4.5R; max drawdown about 10–13R.
Honest reading: a small positive edge on one market, per-trade tiny (+0.04 to +0.07R), consistent over train/validation/exam, still unproven forward.

## 10. Correction after an external review and re-test (20 Nov 2026, late)
An independent reviewer flagged missing costs (stop-gap slippage, trailing-stop slippage, financing, commission). Re-testing (`ftmo/cost_stack.py`, `ftmo/stop_1min.py`, summary in `ftmo/RESULT.md`):
- The 5-minute backtest fills trailing-stop exits at a level computed from the previous bar's best price even if price has already moved beyond that level; a live agent that updates the stop at bar boundaries can only exit at market. With gap-aware fills: 2-year mean R +0.046 -> -0.010 (t -0.9); last 365 days +0.062 -> +0.013 (t 0.8). On 1-minute bars (4,434 trades, Nov 2023 - Nov 2026), updating the stop every 5 minutes (live agent) gives -0.007R (t -0.8) and every minute (native trailing stop) gives -0.002R (t -0.3); last year about +0.013R (t about 0.85).
- With the trailing stop off the engine edge disappears (train -0.006R, validation -0.085R, exam +0.015R). The volatility gate is not the source (gate off still positive in the idealised backtest).
- Live entries fill near plan (mean adverse slippage -0.009R over 8 fills). Exit fills are not logged, so live exits cannot be compared yet.
- FTMO simulation not rerun: with about zero realistic edge there is no reason to pay for a challenge. Earlier pass rates (section 6) are void.
Current status of the claim: the +0.04 to +0.07R edge is not supported once execution is modelled realistically. The forward test (idealised shadow replay) measures the same idealisation, so it cannot validate the live edge by itself; log real exit fills.
Reviewer points that were wrong/overstated: exam t=4.28 is not subject to the search's luck bar (it is a fresh test of one pre-selected rule; its weaknesses are one year, one regime, one instrument); phase-2 pass rates were already conditional. Reviewer points confirmed: cost stack, open-trade drawdown, non-overlapping starts, sensitivity tests.
