# Ideas round 1 - blind cross-scoring packet (22 Nov 2026)
Three anonymous sets, labelled X, Y, Z. Score ONLY the sets that are not yours, using the rubric in docs/IDEAS_ROUND_1.md (each proposal 0-2 on: source real and correctly described, power/effect size honest, pre-registerable, novel, feasible; /10; session = best proposal + 1 if calibrated; -2 per invented citation; -1 padding). Check citations yourself if you can; say "unverified" if you cannot. 150 words per set max. Do not guess who wrote what.

## Set X
- X1. NFP and CPI release days, long NAS100: buy at the open of the 08:25 New York bar, exit at the close of the 15:55-16:00 bar; skip FOMC days, early closes, missing bars; no stop; n about 128. Source: Savor and Wilson, "How Much Do Investors Care About Macroeconomic Risk? Evidence From Scheduled Economic Announcements" (journal: unsure; reported mean announcement-day excess return roughly +10 to +15 bp). Power: SD about 140 bp, SE 12.4 bp, detectable 35 bp net; at 12 bp power about 3%, at 25 bp about 22%. Net cost about 0.8-2 bp. Kill: not (one-sided t >= 2.8 and net mean >= +1 bp and >= 2 of 3 thirds positive and event-day minus other-day mean >= 0). Prior 4-5%. N +1.
- X2. Last full trading day before each NYSE holiday, long NAS100: 09:30 New York bar open to 15:55-16:00 close; n about 48. Source: Ariel, "High Stock Returns before Holidays: Existence and Evidence on Possible Causes" (journal: unsure). Power: SD about 85 bp, SE 12.3 bp, detectable 34 bp; at decayed 20 bp power about 12%. Net cost 1-2 bp. Kill rule as X1. Prior 5%. N +1.
- Third slot empty: overnight (needs about 8.5 bp gross a night against about 2.5 bp costs), turn-of-month (power about 5%), VIX/yield/dollar/HYG-conditioned daily rules (no credible published effect), cross-index lead-lag (no credible citation).

## Set Y
- Y1. Overnight versus intraday: long NAS100 from the 16:00 New York close bar to the 09:30 open bar, one trade a night (Friday to Monday as one), no stop, net of half-spreads and overnight financing. Source: Kelly and Clark, "Returns in trading versus non-trading hours: the difference is day and night" (journal: Journal of Investing or similar; unsure). Power: about 1,400 nights, SD about 60 bp, SE 1.6 bp, detectable about 4.5 bp; costs 2-3 bp so needs net +1 bp. Prior 5%. Kill: net mean < +1.0 bp or t < 2.8 or positive in fewer than 2 of 3 thirds. N +1.
- Y2. Pre-FOMC drift: long NAS100 from 24 hours before the scheduled FOMC statement to 5 minutes before it. Source: Lucca and Moench, "The Pre-FOMC Announcement Drift", Journal of Finance 2015 (about 49 bp average in 1994-2011; weaker since). Power: about 40 events, SD about 80 bp, SE 12 bp, detectable about 30 bp or more. Prior 10%. Kill: t < 2.8 or net mean < +10 bp. N +1. Science-only (FTMO forbids entries within 2 hours of Fed decisions).
- Y3. Turn-of-the-month: long from the 15:55 bar of the last trading day before month-end to the close of the third trading day of the new month, one trade a month. Source: Lakonishok and Smidt, "Are seasonal anomalies real?", Review of Financial Studies 1988. Power: about 66 months, SD about 250 bp, SE 31 bp, detectable about 85 bp. Prior 3%. Kill: t < 2.8 or net mean < +20 bp. N +1. Submitter recommends probably not spending an N on it.
