## Why a new look needs NEW data
F1 passed on 2021-2026 (+45 bp, t 2.95) and failed its replication on 2016-2021 (+18 bp, t 1.27). Both windows have now been seen, so pooling them is not a new test. Genuinely unseen data is: (1) 2010 to 2016-11 (about 44 scheduled FOMC decisions), and (2) every decision from 9 Dec 2026 onward (8 a year).

## Power, stated before any further look (SD of one F1 event = 100 bp, from the 43 events; effect sizes are what we could be chasing)
| True effect | events for 80% power at t >= 2.0 | years of FOMC meetings |
|---|---|---|
| 45 bp (the 2021-2026 estimate) | 40 | 5 |
| 30 bp | 90 | 11 |
| 18 bp (the 2016-2021 estimate) | 249 | 31 |
| 10 bp | 808 | 101 |
Honest consequence: 2010-2016 alone (about 44 events) can only confirm an effect near 45 bp; it cannot rule out 18 bp. A "properly powered" test of a modest effect would need about 250 events (31 years), which no 5-minute data we can get covers. A daily-close version of the same idea (previous close or 14:00 to the decision) could use decades of free daily data, but is a different trade (the timestamp 14:00 to 14:00 is lost) and would be a separate registration.

## UPDATE 23 Nov 2026 09:5x: free data found (Joe asked "can you get what you need for free from Kaggle")
Kaggle is blocked from the session, but the same need is met by a free public GitHub repo, FutureSharks/financial-data, which holds OANDA NAS100_USD 1-minute files for 2005 to 2020 (monthly CSVs, columns time, close, high, low, open, volume). OANDA is the same broker as our live feed. Checked without looking at returns: (1) timestamps are UTC (tick-volume peak at 14:30 in winter and 13:30 in summer = 09:30 New York); (2) bars exist only when a tick arrived (median gap 1 minute but gaps occur), so 5-minute bars are built from the 1-minute bars (open of the first bar in the window, close of the last); (3) no spread column, so a flat half-spread assumption is needed (proposed 2.5 bp per leg base, sensitivity 1 bp and 5 bp; the F1 effect is 15 to 45 bp so cost choice barely matters). Raw files stay OUT of the public repo (licence unclear); only code and results go in. Local copy: /home/claude/fs_probe/financial-data (not in any repo).
Catch found while checking the Fed's own pages: the FOMC statement time was NOT always 14:00 New York. The Fed changed statement times in March 2013 (press release of 13 March 2013), and before that several meetings were single-day or released at other times (for example the 2012 schedule has one-day meetings). F1's "14:00 to 14:00" anchor is only correct for scheduled meetings from March 2013 on. So the CLEAN new pool is the scheduled decisions from 2013-03-20 to 2016-11-02, about 30 events (2013: 7, 2014: 8, 2015: 8, 2016 to 2 Nov: 7; 2013-03-20 onward). Power at 30 events: only a mean near 52 bp is detectable at 80% power and t 2.0. Pre-2013 events (2005 to 2012, about 60 more) could be added only with each statement time verified from the Fed's statements and the 24-hour window anchored on that time; that is a separate, larger registration (more events, not a clean copy of F1).
