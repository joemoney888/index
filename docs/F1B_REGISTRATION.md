# F1b: a properly powered look at the pre-FOMC drift (registered 23 Nov 2026, Claude Sonnet 5.5; NOT RUN: waits for data)
Cap lifted by the PM (project manager) at 09:51 UTC, 23 Nov 2026 ("lift the cap and register a new, properly powered look at it"). N: 941,548 + 1 = **941,549** pre-created here. Further N is not capped by this decision but each new test still needs its own registration.

## Why a new look needs NEW data
F1 passed on 2021-2026 (+45 bp, t 2.95) and failed its replication on 2016-2021 (+18 bp, t 1.27). Both windows have now been seen, so pooling them is not a new test. Genuinely unseen data is: (1) 2010 to 2016-11 (about 44 scheduled FOMC decisions), and (2) every decision from 9 Dec 2026 onward (8 a year).

## Power, stated before any further look (SD of one F1 event = 100 bp, from the 43 events; effect sizes are what we could be chasing)
| True effect | events for 80% power at t >= 2.0 | years of FOMC meetings |
|---|---|---|
| 45 bp (the 2021-2026 estimate) | 40 | 5 |
| 30 bp | 90 | 11 |
| 18 bp (the 2016-2021 estimate) | 249 | 31 |
| 10 bp | 808 | 101 |
Honest consequence: 2010-2016 alone (about 44 events) can only confirm an effect near 45 bp; it cannot rule out 18 bp. A "properly powered" test of a modest effect would need about 250 events (31 years), which no 5-minute data we can get covers. A daily-close version of the same idea (previous close or 14:00 to the decision) could use decades of free daily data, but is a different trade (the timestamp 14:00 to 14:00 is lost) and would be a separate registration.

## Rule (frozen now, applied once)
Trade identical to F1: long NAS100 (or the nearest index future or ETF in the vendor data if NAS100 is unavailable, ONE instrument stated before the run) at the open of the 14:00 New York bar on the previous trading day, exit at the close of the 13:55 bar on the decision day. Events: scheduled FOMC decision days only; unscheduled meetings excluded; March 2020 not in this window. Costs: the vendor's quoted bid/ask if present, otherwise the F1 median half-spread of 0.19 bp per leg x1.5 (index futures spreads are about 0.25 index points). Pass on the NEW pool: net mean >= +15 bp AND t >= 2.0 AND positive in at least 2 of 3 equal-count thirds. Pooling with 2016-2021 or 2021-2026 is NOT part of the rule. Result recorded either way.
Meaning of outcomes: pass = F1 survives three windows (two consistent, one weaker): worth a cautious forward paper test; fail = closed as "not detectable at this scale". The forward events (from 9 Dec 2026) are logged as they come but are not tested until at least 40 exist (about 5 years); they are not a project deliverable by 19 Dec.

## UPDATE 23 Nov 2026 09:5x: free data found (the PM asked "can you get what you need for free from Kaggle")
Kaggle is blocked from the session, but the same need is met by a free public GitHub repo, FutureSharks/financial-data, which holds OANDA NAS100_USD 1-minute files for 2005 to 2020 (monthly CSVs, columns time, close, high, low, open, volume). OANDA is the same broker as our live feed. Checked without looking at returns: (1) timestamps are UTC (tick-volume peak at 14:30 in winter and 13:30 in summer = 09:30 New York); (2) bars exist only when a tick arrived (median gap 1 minute but gaps occur), so 5-minute bars are built from the 1-minute bars (open of the first bar in the window, close of the last); (3) no spread column, so a flat half-spread assumption is needed (proposed 2.5 bp per leg base, sensitivity 1 bp and 5 bp; the F1 effect is 15 to 45 bp so cost choice barely matters). Raw files stay OUT of the public repo (licence unclear); only code and results go in. Local copy: /home/claude/fs_probe/financial-data (not in any repo).
Catch found while checking the Fed's own pages: the FOMC statement time was NOT always 14:00 New York. The Fed changed statement times in March 2013 (press release of 13 March 2013), and before that several meetings were single-day or released at other times (for example the 2012 schedule has one-day meetings). F1's "14:00 to 14:00" anchor is only correct for scheduled meetings from March 2013 on. So the CLEAN new pool is the scheduled decisions from 2013-03-20 to 2016-11-02, about 30 events (2013: 7, 2014: 8, 2015: 8, 2016 to 2 Nov: 7; 2013-03-20 onward). Power at 30 events: only a mean near 52 bp is detectable at 80% power and t 2.0. Pre-2013 events (2005 to 2012, about 60 more) could be added only with each statement time verified from the Fed's statements and the 24-hour window anchored on that time; that is a separate, larger registration (more events, not a clean copy of F1).
Status: F1b NOT RUN. The PM decides between (A) run now on the clean 30-event pool (cheap, weak power: a pass means a big effect; a fail means little), (B) first build the verified statement-time table for 2005-2012 and register the 24h-before-statement version on about 90 events (detects about 30 bp), or (C) stop at "promising, unconfirmed".

## DECISION 23 Nov 2026 09:57 (the PM): "do A and B". Both are run, A first.
### A - clean 30-event pool (frozen before running)
Instrument OANDA NAS100_USD 1-minute (GitHub FutureSharks/financial-data), UTC stamps. 5-minute bars built from 1-minute bars (open = first 1-minute open in the 5-minute window; close = last 1-minute close). Trade as F1: long at the open of the 14:00 New York bar on the previous weekday, exit at the close of the 13:55 bar on the decision day. Events: famB/fomc_2013_2016.json (30). Cost: 2.5 bp half-spread per leg (base), also 1.0 and 5.0 bp sensitivities; pass judged at 2.5. Skip an event if either window has no 1-minute bar. Pass: net mean >= +15 bp, t >= 2.0, positive in at least 2 of 3 equal-count thirds. Pooling is not part of the rule. N +1 (already pre-created: 941,549).
### A RESULT (run once, 23 Nov 2026; famB/RESULT_A.json): 30 events, net mean -12.3 bp at 2.5 bp half-spread (t -1.27); -9.3 bp at 1.0 bp, -17.3 bp at 5.0 bp; thirds -7.3 / -25.3 / -4.2 bp. FAIL. (A first attempt produced zero events because of a timestamp-unit bug in my runner, saw no outcome, was fixed and re-run; commit "Fix epoch unit bug".)
### B - pre-2013 events (frozen before running; N +1, pre-created 941,550)
Same data, bar construction, trade and rule as A (long at the open of the 14:00 New York bar on the previous weekday, exit at the close of the 13:55 bar on the decision day; half-spread 2.5 bp per leg with 1.0 and 5.0 sensitivities; pass: net >= +15 bp, t >= 2.0, 2 of 3 equal-count thirds positive). Events: famB/fomc_2005_2013.json, 57 scheduled decision days from Feb 2005 to 30 Jan 2013 with the 8 press-briefing meetings (statement at about 12:30) excluded in advance. Before 20 Mar 2013 the usual statement time was about 14:15 (currencythoughts.com note; the Fed press release of 13 Mar 2013 moved it to 14:00), so the exit at 13:55 is about 20 minutes before the announcement and the window stays clean. Power: about 57 events, SD about 100 bp, so only a mean near 38 bp is detectable at 80% power. 2005-2009 dates are from memory (flagged in the file).
### B RESULT (run once, 23 Nov 2026; famB/RESULT_B.json): only 17 of the 57 events could be tested. All 40 events from 2005 to 2009 were skipped because the free OANDA files have no bar near 14:00 on those days (a coverage gap I did not know about before running; the skip rule was in the frozen spec). Tested: 2010 to Jan 2013 (17 events): net mean -29.4 bp at 2.5 bp half-spread (t -1.35); -26.4 bp at 1.0; -34.3 bp at 5.0; thirds -68.5 / -29.2 / +17.3 bp. FAIL, and badly underpowered (a 17-event test can only see a mean near 69 bp).
