# Ideas round 1 - rules for all participants (GLM, GPT6, Claude)
Goal: new rule ideas the PM can decide on without watching videos. Everyone gets the same prompt and the same rubric. Claude committed its own proposals first (docs/IDEAS_ROUND_CLAUDE.md, 22 Nov 2026 00:15 UTC) and has not read the others' before scoring.
**Reviewers: do NOT open docs/IDEAS_ROUND_CLAUDE.md until you have sent your own proposals.**

## The prompt (same for every reviewer)
Propose up to 3 new rules to test on NAS100 (or SPX500/US30) CFD data we hold: 5-minute bars with spreads, 2021-05-20 to 2026-11-17 (about 1,400 days), plus daily macro series (VIX, 2/5/10-year yields, dollar index, HYG) since 2011. Constraints: paper only; FTMO-style limits are context, not required; costs are real (half-spread each side, financing overnight); the experiment's trial counter N is 941,542 and only +3 more are available (Family A uses +3 of +6; the PM may raise the cap). BFG has no demonstrated edge, so do not assume an edge; and do NOT use ideas mined from our own backtests (the old ORB, mean-reversion, chart-pattern and trend searches already failed; Family A (A5, A6a, A6b) and the Fed family are already on the list, so don't repeat them).
For each proposal give: (1) the exact rule, unambiguous, with times and timezone; (2) the mechanism and a verifiable published source (authors, title, journal, year); say if you are unsure of a citation; (3) expected effect size and the power at our sample size, with arithmetic; (4) net cost per trade; (5) what result kills it (numbers, fixed in advance); (6) your honest prior that it passes; (7) N cost. Maximum about 700 words. No padding. If you have no idea that meets the bar, say so rather than inventing one.

## Scoring rubric (applied by Claude, then each reviewer scores the others' proposals blind, labelled X/Y/Z; the PM decides)
Each proposal 0-2 on each of: (a) **Source real and correctly described** (Claude checks by search; a made-up or misdescribed citation scores 0 and is flagged); (b) **Power and effect size honest** (arithmetic correct, not hand-waved); (c) **Fully pre-registerable** (rule exact, kill rule numeric); (d) **Novel** (not already on the list or failed); (e) **Feasible** with our data, costs and N. A proposal's score is the sum /10; a session score is the best proposal plus 1 if the set is calibrated (honest priors, admits weak power). Penalties: -2 per invented citation; -1 for padding.
Scores go into the scoreboard in docs/HANDOVER_GPT6.md. Claude's own proposals are scored by the two reviewers blind and by Claude last, so the PM can see all three views.
