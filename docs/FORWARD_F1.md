# Forward test of F1 (pre-FOMC drift): registered 23 Nov 2026 by the PM's decision ("forward test"), written by Claude Sonnet 5.5
Paper only. No orders on any account; the job reads prices from the second practice account's data feed and writes a log. Trade: long NAS100, open of the 14:00 New York 5-minute bar on the previous weekday to the close of the 13:55 bar on the decision day, each leg paying half the bar's spread. Events: famB/fomc_forward.json (9 scheduled decisions from 9 Dec 2026 to 8 Dec 2027; a moved date is edited with a note before the event).
Evidence rule, fixed now: no test is run before 40 events exist (about the end of 2031 at 8 a year). At 40 events the pass rule is: net mean >= +15 bp and t >= 2.0 and positive in at least 2 of 3 equal-count thirds. An optional early look at 20 events is for monitoring only and carries no decision. Skipped events (missing bar) are logged as SKIPPED, never replaced. N +0 now (the test itself was counted at 941,550 when F1b was registered); the 40-event test will not add a new N.
Honest expectation: with the older windows negative, the prior that a real +15 bp effect exists is low; this log costs almost nothing, which is why it is kept. The PM can stop it any time by disabling the step in .github/workflows/forward.yml.
