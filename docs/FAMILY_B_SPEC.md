## F1 - Pre-FOMC drift (Lucca and Moench, Journal of Finance 2015)
Events: the 44 FOMC decision days in famB/fomc.json inside the window (statement 14:00 New York). Trade: LONG at the open of the 14:00 bar on the PREVIOUS trading day (24 hours before), exit at the close of the 13:55 bar on the decision day (14:00). Net of spread. No overnight financing is modelled (flagged; a CFD would pay roughly 1 bp); floor for rule (b): net mean >= +15 bp. Skip an event if either bar is missing. Power statement (before running): per-event SD of a 24-hour NAS100 return is about 100 bp, so with about 44 events the detectable mean at t 2.8 is about 42 bp. The published effect (about 49 bp, shrinking after publication) is only just inside that. A fail here means "not detectable", not "absent".

## F2 - Post-announcement continuation
Events: the same 44 days. Predictor: return from the open of the 14:00 bar to the close of the 14:25 bar. Trade: same sign, entered at the open of the 14:30 bar, exited at the close of the 15:55 bar. Net of spread. Floor for rule (b): net mean >= +10 bp. Power: per-event SD of a 90-minute return about 40 bp, detectable mean about 17 bp. Opposite-sign (reversal) is recorded only.

## ORB - Opening-range breakout (Idea 1, N +1, breakout only; the fade is NOT run)
Range = high and low of the three 5-minute bars starting 09:30, 09:35, 09:40 (09:30 to 09:45 New York). Signal = the first 5-minute bar starting from 09:45 up to and including the 11:25 bar that CLOSES beyond the range (above the high: long; below the low: short); once per day; if none, no trade. Entry = open of the next bar (market, adverse half-spread). Stop = the opposite side of the range. Target = 2R from the entry price, where R = entry-to-stop distance. If R is zero or the entry price is already beyond the stop, skip. Exit: stop or target intrabar (gap-aware: a bar opening beyond the stop fills at that open; if one bar touches both stop and target the stop is assumed first), otherwise the close of the 15:55 bar. Exit pays the exiting bar's half-spread. Metric: net R per trade. Skip days with calendar holidays, early closes (famA/calendar.json) or fewer than 78 bars. Rule (b): net mean >= +0.05R. t-statistic is plain (one event per day). Power: about 1,000 trades, SD about 1R, detectable mean about 0.09R.
